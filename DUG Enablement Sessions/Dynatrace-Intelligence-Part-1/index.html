The starting point

The Gen2 troubleshooting pattern

Today, most teams follow the same manual drill when a problem fires:

  1. Open the Problem card
  2. Identify the impacted entity
  3. Navigate to the service overview page
  4. Drill into Details, then Errors, then individual traces
  5. Cross-reference logs, metrics, and dependencies manually
  6. Repeat until you find the root cause — if you find it at all

This works. But it's slow, click-heavy, and depends on tribal knowledge.

Before the tooling

The required mindset shift

Gen3 + proper implementation changes the game — but only if you unlearn old habits.

  • Stop clicking through 10 screens — ask a question instead
  • Stop writing DQL from scratch — describe what you need in plain English
  • Stop hoarding runbooks in wikis — index them so AI can surface them when it matters
  • Proper configuration is the prerequisite — logs, traces, and entities must be stitched together
With the right setup, you get to the problems that matter in almost no time. This is where Dynatrace Intelligence comes in.
Concept

What is Dynatrace Intelligence?

Agentic & generative AI that bridges human language and machine data — integrated directly into your Dynatrace environment.

  • Explore massive environments and boost productivity without mastering DQL
  • AI translates conversational prompts into auto-executing queries
  • Accelerates onboarding — new team members become productive faster
  • Context-aware — provides summaries, root causes, and remediation in place
  • Enterprise-grade — strict RBAC, PII protection, zero model training on your data

The foundation

Configuration prerequisites

For Davis CoPilot to deliver maximum value, ensure these are in place:

  • Deep monitoring of all services with complete K8s visibility
  • Logs ingested with Log Enrichment enabled
  • Logs, traces & entities properly stitched together
  • Process snapshots & proper OneAgent flags turned on
  • Deployment & change management events ingested
  • Optional: Application Security, BizEvents, architecture extensions
