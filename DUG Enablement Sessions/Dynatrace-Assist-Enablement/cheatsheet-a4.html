Prompting best practices

  • Name the entity — service, host, or pod, not "the thing."
  • Give a timeframe — "last 2 hours", "since the deploy."
  • State the goal — a query, an explanation, or next steps.
  • One question at a time — chain follow-ups, don't stack.
  • Paste the real error — exact strings beat paraphrases.
  • Always read the sources — trust, then verify.
  • Iterate — "shorter", "as DQL", "for a manager."
  • Keep every prompt under 10,000 characters.

Weak prompt

"Why is it slow?"

Strong prompt

"Why did the checkout service response time spike in the last 30 minutes, and what should I check first?"

Entity + metric + timeframe + goal = a grounded, cited answer.
